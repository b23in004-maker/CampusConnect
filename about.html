About CampusConnect

A Smart Campus Management System that simplifies student activities, event registrations, resource booking, and campus communication through one centralized platform.

Who We Are

CampusConnect is a web-based platform developed to improve the campus experience for students, faculty, and administrators.

The system provides one place to manage events, reserve campus resources, join student clubs, receive announcements, and access important campus services efficiently.

Mission & Vision

Our goal is to build a smarter digital campus.

Mission

To simplify campus activities through an easy-to-use digital platform that connects students and administration.

Vision

To create a modern smart campus where every service is available online with a single login.
